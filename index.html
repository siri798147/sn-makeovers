Sacred Telugu Pelli Kundalu & Ceremonial Wedding Essentials

In South Indian and Telugu wedding traditions, sacred clay pots (Pelli Kundalu, Gariga Muntha, Irandla Kundalu) represent divine presence, fertility, and lifelong prosperity. Swetha D handcrafts each piece with ceremonial reverence, offering you the choice of:

1. Authentic Traditional Hand Painting

Auspicious depictions of Lord Ganesha, Goddess Lakshmi, sacred Kalasam, peacocks, and traditional temple borders painted in vibrant ritual dyes.

2. Royal Kundan & Zari Stone Work

Dazzling hand-embedded kundan crystals, pearl tassels, and golden beadwork that sparkle under mandap lights and match your bridal jewelry.

How Saree Box Folding Works

No more panicking or spending 45 minutes wrestling with pleats on your big day. With Swetha's signature pre-pleating, you slip into your saree in under 3 minutes!

1

Drop-off or Courier

Hand over your silk, kanjeevaram, or designer sarees 2 to 4 days prior to your occasion.

2

Precision Pleating

Swetha steam presses each fold to your waist & height measurements, crafting up to 13 razor-sharp pleats.

3

Box or Hanger Pack

Secured in premium wrinkle-resistant box packing or hung on steam-padded hangers ready for venue travel.

4

Wear in 3 Minutes!

Step in, tie the inner waist chord, drape the pallu, and walk out looking like a royalty without stress!
