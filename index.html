How Saree Box Folding Works

No more panicking or spending 45 minutes wrestling with pleats on your big day. With Swetha's signature pre-pleating, you slip into your saree in under 3 minutes!

1

Drop-off or Courier

Hand over your silk, kanjeevaram, or designer sarees 2 to 4 days prior to your occasion.

2

Precision Pleating

Swetha steam presses each fold to your waist & height measurements, crafting up to 13 razor-sharp pleats.

3

Box or Hanger Pack

Secured in premium wrinkle-resistant box packing or hung on steam-padded hangers ready for venue travel.

4

Wear in 3 Minutes!

Step in, tie the inner waist chord, drape the pallu, and walk out looking like a royalty without stress!
